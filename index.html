Questions,
answered

Will it really look and sound like me?

Yes. The avatar is trained on your own footage and the voice model on your own recordings. You approve test clips before anything is published, and we retrain until you are happy.

How much of my time does it take?

About twenty minutes of recording once, plus a 45-minute strategy call on Pro and Elite. After that you only approve reels, which usually takes five minutes a week.

Who owns my digital twin?

You do. Your likeness is used only for your content, never for anyone else, and we delete the models on request when you leave.

Which platforms do you make reels for?

Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn video. Every reel is vertical 9:16, with subtitles burned in or as separate files.

Is my data safe?

Source footage and models are stored encrypted, access is limited to your producer, and every reel carries the AI disclosure that platforms require.
